👑 What Is Lurex Kingdom?

Lurex Kingdom is a community-focused Discord server created to bring people together through gaming, Roblox, entertainment, events, giveaways, conversations, and friendships.

Our goal is to create a place where members can have fun, meet new people, participate in activities, and feel like they are genuinely part of a community.

The Kingdom is constantly evolving, with new ideas, events, features, and opportunities being introduced as the community grows.

🎮 Gaming Roblox, games and gaming-related community activities.
🎁 Giveaways Community giveaways and special rewards.
⚡ Events Competitions, activities and community events.
👥 Community Meet people, make friends and enjoy the Kingdom.

Kingdom Guidelines

Keep Lurex Kingdom enjoyable, welcoming and safe for everyone.

✓

Things To Do

✓ Respect other members and treat everyone fairly.
✓ Participate in conversations, events and community activities.
✓ Follow the instructions given by the moderation team.
✓ Use the correct channels for their intended purposes.
✓ Report problems or rule violations to the appropriate staff members.
✓ Help keep the community welcoming and enjoyable for everyone.
✕

Things To Avoid

✕ Do not harass, threaten, bully or target other members.
✕ Do not spam messages, mentions, emojis or unnecessary content.
✕ Do not advertise without permission from the staff team.
✕ Do not attempt to scam, deceive or exploit other members.
✕ Do not intentionally disrupt events or community activities.
✕ Do not abuse bots, permissions, channels or server features.
